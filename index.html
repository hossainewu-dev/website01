Five building blocks for digital trust

Use one on its own or combine them into a single workflow, from first customer contact to the signed record.

Digital Signature

Sign contracts, forms and approvals online. Each signature is tied to the signer and to the exact content, so any later change is detectable.

Discuss digital signature

eKYC

Onboard customers remotely. Capture an identity document, read its data and run verification checks without a branch visit.

Discuss eKYC

Face Verification

Compare a live face capture with a reference photo, with liveness checks to help detect spoofing attempts.

Discuss face verification

PKI

Issue, manage and validate the digital certificates that signatures and secure connections depend on.

Discuss PKI

Visible Digital Seal (VDS)

Add a scannable, cryptographically protected seal to a document so anyone holding the printout can check it is genuine.

Discuss VDS

How the pieces work together

A typical flow for a service that needs verified people and trustworthy documents.

  1. 1

    Identify the person

    eKYC and face verification confirm who is applying.

  2. 2

    Issue credentials

    PKI provides the certificate that binds an identity to a signing key.

  3. 3

    Sign and seal

    Digital signatures approve the record. A VDS makes the printout checkable.

  4. 4

    Verify anywhere

    Anyone can confirm the document is authentic and unchanged.

Built around how each sector works

Processes and regulatory requirements differ by sector. We configure the solution around yours.

Banks and financial institutions

Open accounts and approve loans without asking customers to visit a branch, while keeping a verifiable record of every step.

  • Remote account opening with eKYC
  • Face verification for higher-risk transactions
  • Digitally signed loan and account documents
  • VDS on statements and certificates

Example: remote account opening

  1. Customer captures an ID document and a live photo.
  2. The bank checks the data and the face match.
  3. Customer signs the account agreement online.
  4. The signed agreement is stored and can be verified later.

Telecom operators

Register subscribers accurately and reduce impersonation, from the retail point of sale to self-service channels.

  • SIM registration and subscriber onboarding
  • Face-based identity checks at activation
  • Paperless customer agreements
  • Fewer fraudulent registrations

Example: subscriber registration

  1. Agent or customer scans the ID document.
  2. The live face is matched to the document photo.
  3. Subscriber signs the agreement on screen.
  4. The SIM is activated and the record is kept.

Government organizations

Move approvals and citizen services online, and let anyone check that an official document is genuine.

  • Digitally signed approvals and notices
  • Certificates and licenses that can be verified with VDS
  • Citizen identity checks for online services
  • PKI foundations for secure data exchange

Example: issuing a certificate

  1. An officer approves the application.
  2. The certificate is digitally signed.
  3. A VDS is added to the printable copy.
  4. A recipient scans the seal to confirm it is genuine.
